Illustrative entries to demonstrate the layout. Actual records must come from the backend. The filters and sample entries above are for demonstrating the interface; the live version should use real database records rather than fixed sample counts.

What this page must record

Content changes: creation, edits, publication, unpublishing, archiving and deletion requests.
School management: school-manager submissions, review decisions and approved profile changes.
Account security: login successes and failures, password-reset events, role changes and account suspension.
Administrative actions: settings changes, permission updates and significant system operations.
Change details: actor, timestamp, event ID, affected record, action outcome and before/after values where appropriate.

Important security rules

Append-only records. Normal administrators must not be able to edit or delete historical events.
Server-side recording. Record events on the server, not just in browser JavaScript.
No secrets. Never log passwords, authentication tokens, or other secrets.
Restricted access. Restrict audit access to the platform owner and specifically authorized administrators.
Controlled exports. Make exports permission-controlled and record when an audit export occurs.
Retention & privacy. Apply retention and privacy policies to IP addresses and device information.
