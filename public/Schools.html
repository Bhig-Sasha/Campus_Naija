School overview

The University of Abuja is a federal university located in the Federal Capital Territory, Nigeria. Established in 1988, it offers undergraduate and postgraduate programmes across a wide range of disciplines including sciences, arts, social sciences, management, law, and engineering.

The university operates multiple campuses and is committed to academic excellence, research, and community service. Its strategic location in Abuja provides students with access to Nigeria's political and administrative center.

Faculties and colleges

Faculty of Science Faculty of Arts Faculty of Social Sciences Faculty of Management Sciences Faculty of Law Faculty of Engineering College of Health Sciences Faculty of Education Faculty of Agriculture

Facilities and student life

The university provides on-campus accommodation, libraries, laboratories, sports facilities, and student support services. Campus life includes student associations, cultural events, and religious activities.

Admissions

Admission into the University of Abuja is through UTME and Direct Entry. Applicants must satisfy the general university requirements and specific programme requirements.

Entry requirements

RouteRequirementsStatus
UTME (100 Level) 5 O'Level credits including English and Mathematics; UTME cut-off 180 (sample) Open
Direct Entry (200 Level) A-Level / NCE / ND with minimum Lower Credit; O'Level requirements apply Open
Post-UTME screening Computer-based screening; candidates must upload O'Level results Scheduled
