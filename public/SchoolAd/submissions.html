Submission lifecycle

Every submission follows this workflow. You can prepare changes in drafts, submit them for review, and revise returned content before it is published.

01

Draft

Prepare changes and check the details before submitting.

02

Submitted

The system records the submission and sends it to the review queue.

03

Under review

The platform owner checks accuracy, completeness and supporting evidence.

04

Approved or changes requested

Reviewers approve the content or return it with feedback.

05

Published

Approved content becomes visible on the public school profile when published.

Reviewer feedback

Reviewer comments appear alongside each returned or decided submission. Use the notes to revise the record on its respective page (Courses, Fees, Admissions or Academic Calendar), then re-submit for review.
