Admission information checklist

Every admission notice should include the following information to be eligible for publication.

Application dates

Opening date, closing date and any verified extension.

Entry requirements

O'Level subjects, UTME requirements, Direct Entry and other eligibility criteria.

Eligible programmes

Link the notice to the relevant courses and qualification levels.

Official application link

Direct applicants to a verified institutional or examination portal.

Review and approval

Require platform-owner approval before public publication.

Accuracy matters

The portal provides admission information; it does not itself admit applicants or guarantee admission. Unverified dates must not be shown as confirmed. Representatives can manage notices for their assigned institution only.
