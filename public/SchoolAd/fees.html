Fee record requirements

Every fee record should include the following information to be eligible for publication.

Academic session

Specify the session the fee applies to.

Programme and level

Support programme-specific and level-specific charges.

Source and verification

Record the official source and the date the amount was verified.

Approval status

Track drafts, submitted changes, approved records and published fees.

Important

This module records and publishes fee information; it does not collect payments or confirm a student's payment. Only authorised representatives can edit their assigned school's records.
