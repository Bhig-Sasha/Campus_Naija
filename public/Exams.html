Exam overview

The Unified Tertiary Matriculation Examination (UTME) is conducted by the Joint Admissions and Matriculation Board (JAMB) for admission into Nigerian universities, polytechnics, and colleges of education.

The examination tests candidates on English and three other subjects relevant to their chosen course of study.

Purpose

The UTME serves as the primary entrance examination for Nigerian tertiary institutions, assessing candidates' readiness for higher education.

Requirements & subjects

Eligibility, subject combinations, syllabuses, registration documents, and examination-day instructions.

Eligibility

Candidates must possess a minimum of five O'Level credits including English Language and Mathematics, obtained in not more than two sittings. Candidates awaiting results may also apply.

Subject combinations

Course groupRequired subjectsNotes
Science English, Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry For engineering, medicine, sciences
Social Sciences English, Mathematics, Economics, Government For economics, political science, etc.
Arts English, Literature, Government, CRS/IRS For law, languages, arts

Examination-day instructions

Bring your JAMB registration slip

You must present your printed registration slip and a valid ID to the examination centre.

Arrive early

Candidates should arrive at the examination centre at least 30 minutes before the scheduled time.

No electronic devices

Mobile phones and other electronic devices are not allowed in the examination hall.

Registration documents

National ID or passport O'Level results (or awaiting) JAMB registration slip
