Admission overview

The University of Abuja invites applications from suitably qualified candidates for admission into its undergraduate programmes for the 2026/2027 academic session through the Unified Tertiary Matriculation Examination (UTME).

Eligible applicants

Candidates who sat for the 2026 UTME and obtained a minimum score of 180, with five O'Level credits including English Language and Mathematics in not more than two sittings.
